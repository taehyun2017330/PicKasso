## Design Context

### Users
Brand owners, founders, marketers, and designers use PicKasso to explore AI-generated image directions for a brand. They are comparing boards, selecting useful visual signals, and branching toward stronger directions without needing to understand the underlying generation system.

### Brand Personality
Plainspoken, focused, and editorial. The product should feel like a calm creative workspace: helpful enough to orient users, but restrained enough that the images and decisions stay central.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, minimal, and work-focused with quiet typography, neutral surfaces, and small green accents. Avoid marketing-page language, decorative AI styling, and vague process copy that describes the system instead of the user's current decision.

### Design Principles
- Explain the current screen before explaining future workflow.
- Keep guidance tied to visible controls and layouts.
- Use concrete product terms consistently: brand, exploration, board, grid, cards, selection.
- Let the AI guide handle AI status; sidebar steps should clarify user action.
- Keep copy short enough to scan while comparing images.
